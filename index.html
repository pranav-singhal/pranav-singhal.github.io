Bringing forward deployed engineering to India

The story

A forward deployed engineer works inside the customer's business and keeps building until the software runs in production. I was the first engineering hire at Hyde, a venture-backed startup that deploys Palantir inside India's largest enterprises, and I led deployments at the country's largest e‑commerce and automotive companies.

I also built a multilingual AI voice agent for one of India's biggest brands. It handled about 40,000 calls in its first two weeks and produced a measurable lift in sales.

Why now

Forward deployed engineering is one of the fastest growing roles in AI, and India is emerging as a major hub for it. Most enterprise AI pilots never reach production. This work shows what it takes to get them there.

What I can show

  • First engineering hire at Hyde, working on its two biggest accounts
  • A voice agent built on LiveKit, Sarvam and Cartesia that took about 40,000 calls in two weeks
  • The engineering lead on a large-scale data migration for India's largest e‑commerce company
  • An LLM pipeline that went through recorded sales calls at the automotive company to find interested buyers the sales team had dropped

Getting a startup through a SOC 2 audit with AI coding agents

The story

Alongside my engineering role I led Hyde's security and compliance programme and took the company through its SOC 2 Type 1 audit.

I did the hands-on work with AI coding tools: writing the infrastructure code, fixing failing security controls, and preparing for the auditor interviews.

Why now

Startups are under pressure to prove their security to enterprise customers quickly, and the industry is asking how far AI can be trusted in compliance work. This is a first-hand account of where AI speeds up an audit, and where human judgement and an independent auditor still matter.

What I can show

  • A completed SOC 2 Type 1 audit, run by an independent audit firm
  • Infrastructure code and security control fixes written with AI coding tools
  • The interview preparation I built for the auditor's deep-dive sessions
  • A first-hand view of where AI helped in the audit and where it did not

Earlier work, with public numbers

2.7M+installs of Postman's VS Code extension

The sign-in behind Postman's VS Code extension

At Postman I built the sign-in for the VS Code extension: a PKCE-based OAuth flow that brings secure login into the editor. Signing in is required, so every user of the extension passes through it.

Sources: VS Code Marketplace install count, November 2026. Postman reported over 1.1 million downloads in the year the extension became generally available (The Postman Drop, December 2024).
